## Leaf Removal
Sales (after lead is qualified)

“For leaf removal we have 2 options. Option one is Full service cleanup and option 2 is a curbside pickup. Our full service leaf removal pricing is an hourly rate. We charge $190 minimum which includes the first man hour and the dump fee. If the job takes more than 1 man hour the additional time is $75 per man hour. The standard full service cleanup includes front and backyard and all landscape beds.”



### Q: What will the total price be?

A: “The total price is based on the total time spent on the job. So for example if we spent 2 man hours the total would be $265.00”

### Q: Can you give me an estimate/approximate price?

A: “Yes! However, it can be hard to guess just by looking at it. If you can send us some pictures and I can take a look and give you an guess. Just keep in mind the total is still based on the time spent. So if it’s under my guess it’ll be less and if it’s over it’ll be more.”

### Q: Can I cap the time to meet my budget? 

A: “Unfortunately we can’t cap the time because we wouldn’t want to leave you with a half done job and a big pile of leaves. What we can do is only do one area for example we can skip the landscape beds and backyard and only do the front yard which will save on time.”
